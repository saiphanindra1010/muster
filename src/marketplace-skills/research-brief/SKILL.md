# Research brief

## Shape

1. **Question**: restate what is being decided, in one or two lines.
2. **Bottom line**: the answer up front, with confidence (high / medium / low).
3. **Key findings**: 3-6 bullets, each with a source link.
4. **Risks and open questions**: what could invalidate the answer.
5. **Sources**: numbered list of the links actually used.

## Rules

- Search first; skim at least three independent sources.
- Prefer primary sources (docs, papers, changelogs) over blog summaries.
- Quote numbers with their date - rates, prices and versions go stale.
- When sources disagree, say so and explain which one to trust and why.
- Say "not verified" instead of guessing.
